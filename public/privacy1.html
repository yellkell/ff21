FIRE FIGHT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

FIRE FIGHT ("the game", "we") is a virtual-reality game developed by yellkell. This policy explains what information the game handles, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We built the game to run on as little personal data as possible: it has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and it never asks for your name, email address, or any real-world identity.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous player ID. On first launch the game generates a random identifier on your device. It is not derived from you, your device, or your Meta account, and cannot be used to identify you outside the game.
  • Callsign. A display name you type yourself, shown on leaderboards and to other players. Choose whatever you like; it does not need to be (and should not be) your real name.
  • Gameplay records. Scores, ranks, experience points, campaign completion times, in-game currency and unlocked cosmetics, stored against the anonymous player ID so leaderboards and your progression work across sessions.
  • Matchmaking data. Temporary connection-signalling records (lobby entries and network negotiation data) used to connect players peer-to-peer for multiplayer matches. These are transient and routinely deleted.
  • Voice chat (microphone). With your permission, the game transmits your microphone audio live to the other players in your session (multiplayer matches and the social club). Voice audio is relayed in real time only — it is never recorded, stored, or analysed by us. You can mute yourself at any time, mute or block other players, or disable voice chat entirely in Settings.
  • Safety reports. If you use the in-game "Report a problem" tool, the text you type is stored together with your callsign and anonymous player ID so we can act on it and correlate repeat reports.
  • Local settings. Preferences (volumes, mute states, cosmetics, tutorial completion) are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Purchases

FIRE FIGHT 2 sells its in-game currency, iron-dollars, for real money through Stripe. Payment details (card numbers, billing address, email) are entered on Stripe's own checkout page and handled under Stripe's privacy policy; the game and its servers never receive them. What we keep is a ledger entry against your anonymous player identity: which pack was bought, how many iron-dollars it carried, the amount and currency, the date, and Stripe's reference for the session — enough to deliver what you paid for, to prove it was delivered, and to answer a dispute. Nothing in it identifies you by name. See the terms of purchase.

What we do NOT collect

  • No real names, email addresses, or contact details.
  • No Meta account information or friends lists.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • No recordings of your voice, play space, camera, or surroundings.
  • No body, hand, eye, or fitness data beyond the live controller and headset positions needed to play, which are processed in the moment and never stored.
  • No payment information — the game is free and has no purchases.

How we use information

  • To run multiplayer matches, leaderboards and progression.
  • To let you talk to other players over live voice chat.
  • To review safety reports and keep the community safe.

We do not sell any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

  • Other players see your callsign, your avatar, your leaderboard entries and — while you are in a session together — your live voice and in-game movements.
  • Service providers. Gameplay records and matchmaking data are stored with Google Firebase (Google LLC), which hosts the game's backend on our behalf. Google processes this data only to provide the hosting service.
  • We share nothing else with anyone, except where required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • Matchmaking records are transient and deleted in routine cleanup.
  • Gameplay records persist so long as the leaderboards do.
  • You can request deletion of your leaderboard entries and any other server-side data at any time via the in-game report tool ("please delete my data" plus your callsign) or through our Discord community. We will remove it within 30 days.
  • Local settings can be cleared by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling.

Children

FIRE FIGHT is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game collects no real-world identity from anyone. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through a free-text field (callsign or report), contact us via the channels above and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the "last updated" date above. Material changes will also be announced in the game's Discord.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or data requests: use the in-game Report a problem tool (Settings menu), or reach the developer directly on the FIRE FIGHT Discord.
