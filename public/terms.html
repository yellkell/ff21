FIRE FIGHT 2 — Terms of Purchase

Last updated: 15 September 2026

These terms cover buying iron-dollars, the in-game currency of FIRE FIGHT 2 ("the game"), developed by yellkell. By completing a purchase you agree to them. Playing the game costs nothing and everything in the store can be earned by playing; buying iron-dollars is a shortcut, never a requirement.

What you are buying

  • Iron-dollars are a limited licence to unlock cosmetic items inside the game. They are not money, have no cash value, and cannot be exchanged for money or anything outside the game. Inside the game's pub a coin can be dropped and picked up by another player; that is play, not a transfer of anything with value outside the game.
  • The pack, the number of iron-dollars and the price are shown before you pay. Prices include applicable taxes where the law requires it.
  • Iron-dollars are delivered to the anonymous player identity that opened the checkout, and your wallet and locker are kept against that identity in the game's cloud. After buying you can protect that identity with an email address (the store offers this on the paid screen); a protected identity can be recovered on another headset, or after a cleared browser, from the store's BANK board by signing in with a link sent to that email. An identity that was never protected cannot be recovered if the browser's storage is lost, so protect it once you have bought.

Payment

  • Payment is taken by Stripe. The game never sees your card number; Stripe's own terms and privacy policy apply to the payment itself.
  • A purchase is complete when Stripe confirms the payment. The iron-dollars then appear in the game's wallet, normally within seconds, and always the next time the game starts.

Refunds and cancellation

  • Iron-dollars are digital content delivered immediately. By buying them you ask for delivery to start at once and acknowledge that, once delivered, the purchase cannot be cancelled and is not refundable, except where a refund is required by law or where the iron-dollars were never delivered.
  • If a payment was taken and nothing arrived, or you were charged twice, file a report from the game's settings panel or contact us at the address on the privacy page, quoting the date and amount, and we will put it right.

Changes

We may change the packs, prices and what iron-dollars unlock, and may withdraw the store or the game. Iron-dollars already bought keep their value inside the game while the store exists; they are not refunded if it closes.

Age

You must be old enough to enter a contract where you live, or have the permission of a parent or guardian, to buy iron-dollars.
